# Phase 1B — Emerging Cross-Domain Signals

This expansion adds recent evidence on cognitive load, accessibility, collaboration, assistive AR, and conversational/intelligent agents.

## Signals worth carrying forward

### Cognitive load is conditional
Recent systematic and meta-analytic evidence shows substantial heterogeneity. The main project will model cognitive load as conditional on design, task, user, and measurement protocol rather than assign AR a global beneficial or harmful effect.

### Collaboration needs mechanism-level extraction
Recent collaborative-AR reviews call for stronger evidence about interaction elements, collaboration mechanisms, and information representation. Collaboration will therefore be coded at mechanism and deployment level.

### Accessibility belongs in every technical workstream
Accessibility is both a dedicated evaluation topic and an emerging frontier in broad wearable-AR synthesis. Each technical workstream will record population inclusion/exclusion, accessibility evaluation, and capability assumptions.

### Intelligent AR is freshness-sensitive
Recent evidence shows a rapid shift toward LLM-powered conversational/agent architectures. This area will receive a dedicated late-stage search immediately before corpus freeze.

## Schema additions required before bulk primary-study extraction

- evaluation horizon;
- deployment setting;
- accessibility population;
- cognitive-load instrument;
- collaboration topology and role symmetry;
- context-awareness inputs;
- AI/LLM component;
- privacy evaluation;
- failure/adversarial evaluation.
