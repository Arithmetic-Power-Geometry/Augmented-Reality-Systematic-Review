# Phase 1 — Review-of-Reviews Mining

## Purpose

The first empirical phase establishes what previous AR surveys and systematic reviews have already synthesized. It prevents the project from presenting known observations as new gaps and provides a baseline against which the added value of the present research program can be measured.

## Seed evidence set

The initial verified seed set spans:
- field-defining general AR surveys;
- historical updates;
- broad human-computer-interaction synthesis;
- user-study systematic review;
- industrial maintenance reviews;
- AR visualization taxonomy;
- TVCG industrial visual-asset review;
- umbrella review of AR reviews;
- TVCG wearable-AR review;
- TVCG AR training review;
- AR-specific SLAM review;
- recent open/reproducible industrial-maintenance review.

The seed set is intentionally not claimed to be exhaustive. It is the verified starting layer for snowballing and database searches.

## Initial observations to test, not assume

1. AR reviews are highly specialized by application, technology, device class, or evaluation perspective.
2. Technical algorithm reviews and human/user-study reviews are often separated.
3. Dataset and performance analysis exists in focused technical areas such as SLAM, but a field-wide comparability model is not established by the seed set.
4. Large AR reviews already exist; publication count alone cannot constitute novelty.
5. Reproducible review infrastructure is emerging, so open evidence extraction should be treated as a baseline expectation rather than an optional extra.
6. Persistent gaps should be distinguished from historically reported gaps that later became saturated.

## Evidence discipline

A row is marked verified only when bibliographic metadata and the claimed review scope/result are supported by a publisher, indexed bibliographic record, or accessible full text. Search snippets are discovery aids, not sufficient evidence for strong claims.

The coverage matrix is provisional. A value of 1 means the dimension is materially represented in the review according to currently verified evidence; it does not imply exhaustive coverage or quality.
