# EET Gate 1 Freeze — 2026-10-07

This folder is the paper-facing, machine-readable Gate-1 freeze for Evidence Entitlement Theory (EET).

## Status
Gate 1 complete. Prompt 2 has not been run.

## Key findings
- The validated bibliography registry and master BibTeX each contain 212 matching citation keys.
- EET V1 uses a family of partial orders rather than a forced single total order.
- SATISFIED / VIOLATED / UNKNOWN are preserved.
- Evidence updates may retract as well as expand claim entitlement.
- The frontier is the set of maximal licensed claims.
- Minimal obstruction is treated through established minimal-unsatisfied-set / hitting-set machinery where applicable; the mathematics itself is not claimed as new.
- Evidence-gap maps, GRADE, meta-analysis/meta-regression, VOI/EVSI, order theory, hypergraphs/hitting sets, robustness analysis, and causal generalizability are prior-art neighbors. Gate 1 therefore freezes only a candidate integration-level differentiation.
- The old pilot chain c0<c1 is rejected because “can alter” does not logically imply “reduces.”
- Primary endpoints are frozen before Prompt 2: OEE, UEE, macro-F1 claim state, and condition-split macro-F1.
- 200 synthetic evidence worlds are prespecified across ten families, with ground truth generated independently of EET.
- No new human-participant, survey, interview, expert-panel, laboratory, field, clinical, or physical AR experiment is used.
