## Human-confirmed title/abstract selection boundary

Title/abstract screening was organized into immutable work units while computational assistance was restricted to prioritization and nonbinding reviewer prompts. For the first work unit (TA-01; 250 records), the assistance layer integrated explicit AR/MR terminology, false-negative rescue checks, secondary/context-only risk flags, and record-level verification priorities. The executed reviewer-sheet builder produced 38 likely-exclude confirmation prompts, seven rescued/uncertain prompts, 34 priority-verification prompts, and 171 likely-advance confirmation prompts. These states were not treated as eligibility decisions.

The reviewer-facing sheet retained the source ordinal, PMID/DOI, title, abstract, provenance of the assistance signal, suggested controlled reason where applicable, and blank fields for the primary decision, reason code, screener identity, timestamp, evidence note, decision version, second-verification state, and adjudication state. A record entered the formal screening denominator only after a genuine reviewer recorded INCLUDE, EXCLUDE with a frozen controlled reason, or UNCERTAIN. Uncertainty advanced. Computational recommendations never populated reviewer identity or independent-verification fields.

This separation was maintained so that the final study-selection report can state the actual number of reviewers screening each record and report the role of automation independently of human eligibility decisions, consistent with PRISMA 2020 Item 8. Final PRISMA counts are derived only from completed versioned decision ledgers after the corpus-freeze gate passes.
