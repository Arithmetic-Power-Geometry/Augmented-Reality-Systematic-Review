# Run 70 — Search Accounting and Screening-Assistance Separation

The review maintains two distinct provenance layers. First, the formal search registry records the status of every registered source-query cell: 13 PubMed cells were executed and preserved with immutable exports, while 78 non-PubMed cells carry explicit access-limitation records. Thus 91/91 registered cells are accounted for, but only 13/91 are executed; the manuscript must not describe the formal multi-source search as fully executed.

Second, records retrieved through the executed PubMed search are processed through deterministic prescreening and reviewer-workload preparation. Assistance outputs such as likely-advance, likely-exclude, rescue and priority-verification states are quality-control and prioritization signals. They do not populate primary reviewer decisions, reviewer identities, independent-verification fields or adjudication fields.

This separation prevents search completeness, automation coverage and human eligibility decisions from being conflated. Final PRISMA identification and selection counts will be released only after the corresponding evidence ledgers satisfy the corpus-freeze gates.
