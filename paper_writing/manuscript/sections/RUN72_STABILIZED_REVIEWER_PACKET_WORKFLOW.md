# Run 72 — Stabilized Reviewer-Packet Workflow

By TA-04, the screening-assistance workflow had stabilized into a reproducible batch-level sequence: immutable materialization, AR/MR/XR-aware prioritization, exclusion-side false-negative rescue, inclusion-side false-positive risk audit, record-level priority verification, and reviewer-packet construction. The process deliberately preserves ambiguity rather than converting computational signals into eligibility decisions. In particular, records describing augmented virtuality, image augmentation, optical AR systems, mixed reality or digital-twin registration are routed to human confirmation when AR materiality is uncertain. All primary reviewer, verification and adjudication fields remain blank until genuine screening occurs.
