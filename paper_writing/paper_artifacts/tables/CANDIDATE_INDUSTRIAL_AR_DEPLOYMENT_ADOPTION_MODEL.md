# Candidate Industrial AR Deployment and Adoption Model

Represent industrial AR evidence as:
I = (Process, Worker, ARFunction, Device, Environment, Integration, Outcome, Exposure, Deployment, Organization)

## Process classes
assembly; maintenance/repair; inspection/quality control; training; logistics; remote assistance; monitoring; planning.

## Deployment ladder
I0 concept/demonstrator;
I1 laboratory prototype;
I2 controlled industrial-like task;
I3 realistic task with representative workers;
I4 in-situ field trial on operational equipment/process;
I5 repeated operational deployment integrated with workflow/IT;
I6 sustained organizational adoption across time/sites/worker groups.

## Outcome classes
- task: completion time, errors, rework, accuracy, quality;
- worker: workload, usability, situation awareness, learning, ergonomics;
- system: tracking, latency, reliability, battery, connectivity;
- workflow: authoring/setup, integration, downtime, remote-expert coordination;
- organizational: training burden, acceptance, cost, scalability, governance;
- safety: distraction, visibility, physical hazards, failure consequences.

## Prior evidence
- Palmarini et al. 2018: 30 primary maintenance studies; hardware/software/solution fragmentation and maturity limitations.
- Wang et al. 2016: broad AR assembly survey covering planning, ergonomics, guidance and training.
- Gattullo et al. 2022: industrial visual-assets systematic review across maintenance, assembly and training.
- Baroroh et al. 2021: AR in smart manufacturing from human-computational-intelligence perspective.
- Ho et al. 2022: AR manufacturing and Quality Control 4.0 review.
- Breitkreuz et al. 2022: 89 remote-maintenance articles; limited prototype comparability and transferability to industrial professionals.
- Masood & Egger 2020, Loizeau et al. 2021, Zigart & Schlund 2022 already present in master.bib provide adoption/field/user evidence.

## Core rules
1. Lab task speed != industrial productivity.
2. Representative worker evidence is distinct from student/novice evidence.
3. One successful field trial != sustained organizational adoption.
4. Technical usability != integration feasibility.
5. Industrial maturity requires environment, workflow, IT/data, safety, worker and organizational evidence.
6. Report task benefit and deployment burden together.
