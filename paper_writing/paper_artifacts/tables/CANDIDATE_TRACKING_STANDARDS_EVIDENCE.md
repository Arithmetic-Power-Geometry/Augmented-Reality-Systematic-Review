# Candidate Cross-Device Tracking and Standards Evidence

| Source | Layer | Evidence relevant to review |
|---|---|---|
| Hu et al. 2025 | tracking/benchmark/reproducibility | five commercial XR devices; 16 synchronized scenarios; APE/RPE; public hardware/software/data pipeline |
| Makamara & Adolph 2022 | standards/interoperability | maps XR standardization organizations/specifications; interoperability and common terminology are recurring themes |
| IEEE 2048.101-2023 | normative standard | mobile AR software framework, components, integration, functional/performance requirements and test methods |

## Candidate synthesis
Commercial-device tracking should not be treated as a fixed hardware property. The available benchmark evidence supports conditioning tracking performance on **device × sensor stack × environment × motion regime × metric × ground truth**.

Standards/interoperability should likewise be separated into:
1. terminology/taxonomy;
2. software/API/framework integration;
3. data/file/stream representation;
4. virtual-real object interoperability;
5. quality/performance metrics and test methods;
6. safety/accessibility/governance requirements.

This is pre-freeze evidence and will be recomputed from the final corpus.
