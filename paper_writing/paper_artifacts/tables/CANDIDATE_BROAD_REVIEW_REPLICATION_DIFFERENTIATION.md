# Candidate Prior-Review Differentiation — Broad Reviews and Replication

| Prior work | Scope/evidence | What it means for the TVCG review |
|---|---|---|
| Arefin et al. 2025 | 2,167 IEEE ISMAR/IEEE VR papers, 2010–2024; <4% identifiable replication studies | reproducibility/replication must be a first-class evidence dimension |
| Angra et al. 2025 | 22-year AR/VR bibliometric + systematic synthesis | prevents claiming novelty merely from being “broad” or “longitudinal” |
| Balado et al. 2026 | 96 journal papers on 3D as-built/XR workflows | strong comparator for sensing, 3D acquisition, modeling, visualization and interaction |
| Liu et al. 2026 | 27 peer-reviewed papers in computer-graphics education | shows evaluation fragmentation even within one educational subdomain |

## Differentiation that remains defensible
The proposed review should **not** claim novelty because it is broad, systematic, longitudinal, or taxonomy-based. Its differentiating contribution must instead be demonstrated through the integrated evidence architecture:

**D,T,H,S,A,M,E,U,R + Evidence Cube + C0–C3 comparability + study-family provenance + replication/reproducibility audit + contradiction/persistent-gap gates + researcher decision map.**

This is a candidate positioning statement until the final review-of-reviews comparison is frozen.
