# Candidate Temporal Result — Occlusion Handling Evolution

**Pre-freeze synthesis; not yet a final systematic frequency claim.**

| Period | Representative validated evidence | Main mechanism/evaluation shift |
|---|---|---|
| 2005 | Wang, Kumar & Sharma | foreground/background segmentation + projective geometry |
| 2010 | Tian, Guan & Wang | interactive segmentation + real-time object tracking |
| 2022 | Li et al. | monocular SLAM/depth densification + CAD depth fusion for assembly |
| 2026 | Fan, Au & Spiegel | perceptual consequences of stereopsis–occlusion conflict; comfort + task performance |

## Candidate interpretation
The occlusion problem has expanded from a primarily rendering/geometry problem into a **system-and-human problem**. Modern evaluation must therefore distinguish at least:
1. geometric/depth correctness;
2. runtime/latency;
3. scene/device assumptions;
4. task performance;
5. perceptual comfort.

If confirmed in the frozen corpus, this provides a useful example of why algorithm-only comparison is insufficient for AR.
