# Candidate Safety Contradiction — Situational Awareness Depends on Content and Task

**Pre-freeze synthesis; do not interpret as a pooled effect.**

| Study | Context | Safety/awareness signal |
|---|---|---|
| Aromaa et al. 2020 | N=19; seated wooden-block task; minimal AR content | HMD AR did not markedly reduce surrounding awareness under these constrained conditions; physical load remained relevant |
| Chaudhari et al. 2025 | N=100 industry craft workers; full-scale piping assembly; introduced hazard/change | non-AR users detected changes faster; higher AR model detail significantly worsened change detection |
| Cardenas et al. 2026 | navigation with manipulated environmental/HMD clutter and transparency | explicitly tests efficiency, safety and mental-demand consequences of display/environment design |

## Candidate interpretation
The literature does **not** support the scalar claim “AR HMDs are safe” or “AR HMDs reduce awareness.” A more defensible model is:

**safety effect = f(task mobility, hazard salience, virtual-content density, display clutter/transparency, age/experience, environment).**

This candidate contradiction should be tested formally in NEXT-16 after corpus freeze.
