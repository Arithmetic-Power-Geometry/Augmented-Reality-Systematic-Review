## Batch-1 interpretation

The parallel discovery demonstrates that the planned evidence schema can accommodate technical, human, security, accessibility and industrial primary evidence. It also exposes important coverage gaps in this first batch: registration/calibration, occlusion/depth, scene understanding, energy/thermal, safety, standards/interoperability, adaptive AR, GenAI/agents and healthcare require dedicated discovery expansion.

No candidate in this table is counted as an included systematic-review study until NEXT-03 screening.
