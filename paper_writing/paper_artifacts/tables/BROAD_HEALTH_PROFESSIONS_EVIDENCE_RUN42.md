| Evidence layer | Validated anchor | Source-level scale | Implication for this review |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Review-of-reviews | Nikolaidis 2022 | 47 AR reviews | breadth/taxonomy alone is not a sufficient contribution |

## Claim boundary
Counts above are properties of secondary reviews, not counts in this project's primary corpus. Mixed-XR evidence is never treated as AR evidence unless the AR component is separable.
