# Candidate Result — Bystander Privacy Progression

**Pre-freeze synthesis.**

| Study | Privacy framing | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Denning et al. 2014 | recording acceptability and permission around AR glasses | 12 field sessions; 31 bystanders |
| O'Hagan et al. 2022 | awareness/consent needs for everyday always-on AR sensing | online survey N=102 |
| Corbett et al. 2023 | system-level protection of bystander visual data | BystandAR privacy-preserving AR system |

## Candidate interpretation
The literature moves from documenting **bystander concern** toward specifying **awareness/consent requirements** and then implementing **privacy-preserving mechanisms**. Contemporary 2026 consent-driven smart-glasses work has also been discovered but remains outside master.bib until full bibliographic metadata are validated.

This progression should be tested against the complete privacy/governance corpus before becoming a final result.
