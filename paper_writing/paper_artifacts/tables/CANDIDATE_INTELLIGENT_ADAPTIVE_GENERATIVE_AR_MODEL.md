# Candidate Intelligent, Adaptive, and Generative AR Evidence Model

Represent intelligent/adaptive AR as:
I = (AIFunction, InputContext, AdaptationTarget, ModelClass, Loop, EvidenceMaturity, HumanOutcome, SystemCost, SafetyPrivacy, Reproducibility)

## AI-function families
I1 perception/recognition: object, scene, pose, activity, intent;
I2 prediction/decision: next action, hazard, recommendation, planning;
I3 adaptive interaction: modality/interface/content/pacing selection;
I4 conversational assistance: speech/NLP/dialogue/contextual agent;
I5 generative assistance: text/image/3D/instruction/content generation;
I6 immersive analytics: AI-supported analysis/visual reasoning;
I7 robotics/HRI: intent communication, control, planning, feedback.

## Adaptation targets
content; modality; layout/visualization; guidance level; task sequence; conversational response; accessibility; safety intervention; resource/compute allocation.

## Evidence-maturity states
M0 concept/proposal;
M1 offline model evaluation;
M2 functional prototype;
M3 controlled user evaluation;
M4 realistic-task evaluation;
M5 field evaluation;
M6 repeated/longitudinal deployment;
M7 independently reproduced/replicated evidence.

## Core rules
- AI sophistication does not imply evidence maturity.
- Model accuracy does not imply user benefit.
- Personalization/adaptation requires a defined sensed state, decision policy, changed output, and evaluated consequence.
- Conversational AR is not synonymous with generative AI.
- Generative content quality, spatial correctness, latency, hallucination/error, safety/privacy and human outcome are separate variables.

## Prior-art positioning
- Bassyouni & Elhajj 2021: 29 AR+AI robotics papers.
- Ramtohul & Khedo 2025: 15 adaptive multimodal MAR studies retained from 221 non-duplicate records; AI/ML dynamic adaptation identified as underexplored.
- Wu et al. 2026: 44 conversational-agent AR studies (2007-2025); speech dominant and HMDs common.
- Mustapha & Hashim 2026: 30-paper AR+AI review under the Augmented Intelligence Reality label.
- Wang et al. 2026: 43-study immersive analytics+AI review; field characterized as early-stage.
- Li et al. 2026: AR/AI spatial-neglect review already present in master bibliography; domain-specific hybrid evidence.

## Contribution restraint
Do not claim a new general AI-in-AR taxonomy. The manuscript contribution is cross-layer comparability: AI function -> adaptation target -> evidence maturity -> human/system outcome -> safety/privacy -> reproducibility.
