# Candidate Result — Device Effects Are Task- and Time-Conditional

**Pre-freeze synthesis.**

| Study | Device comparison | Key implication |
|---|---|---|
| Aschenbrenner et al. 2019 | HMD, tablet, projection AR, paper, phone support | device effects interact with single-user vs collaborative setting; N=160 |
| Derby & Chaparro 2022 | multiple AR device/application types | mobile interaction familiarity and HMD comfort create different usability constraints |
| Leins et al. 2024 | head-mounted vs handheld AR | guided-assembly outcomes require device-specific performance/cognitive-load analysis |
| Gürerk et al. 2025 | AR HMD vs smartphone, repeated repair task | no immediate time advantage; stronger improvement over time for HMD group; initial quality advantage diminishes |

## Candidate interpretation
There is no defensible universal “best AR device” without conditioning on task, interaction, experience and outcome. Device comparison should therefore preserve:

**device × task × collaboration mode × experience/repetition × performance × quality × workload × usability/comfort.**

This is another reason the project should use a comparability graph rather than a global leaderboard.
