# Algorithm — Educational AR Evidence Promotion Gate

Input: educational AR study s and proposed claim c
Output: PROMOTE, QUALIFY, or HOLD

1. Identify learner population, prior knowledge and educational level.
2. Identify subject/domain and learning objective.
3. Extract pedagogy and scaffolding, not only the AR technology.
4. Identify the AR function that could plausibly affect learning.
5. Record intervention duration, frequency and novelty exposure.
6. Record comparator: no-treatment, conventional material, non-AR digital, alternative immersive medium, or active pedagogy.
7. Classify each outcome L1-L11 separately.
8. Record measurement time: immediate, repeated, delayed, transfer, authentic/field, longitudinal.
9. Separate motivation/engagement from cognitive and skill outcomes.
10. Record cognitive-load construct and instrument separately from achievement.
11. Check whether retention or transfer is actually measured before using those terms.
12. Check moderators before importing a pooled meta-analytic effect into a subgroup.
13. Promote “AR improves learning” only as a qualified population/domain/pedagogy/outcome/time-horizon statement.
14. HOLD durable-learning claims based only on immediate post-tests.
15. HOLD mechanism claims from simple medium-vs-medium comparisons unless design features are isolated.
