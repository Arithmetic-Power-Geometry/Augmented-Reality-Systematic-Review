# Algorithm — Intelligent/Adaptive AR Evidence-Maturity Gate

Input: candidate intelligent-AR study s
Output: AI-function class, adaptation evidence, maturity state M0-M7, allowed synthesis claim

1. Identify the actual AI function; do not infer AI from marketing terminology.
2. Identify model class and inputs/context sensed.
3. If adaptation is claimed, identify the decision/policy and the output that changes.
4. Record whether evaluation is offline, prototype, controlled-user, realistic-task, field, longitudinal, or independently reproduced.
5. Separate model metrics from system metrics and human outcomes.
6. Record baseline/comparator and whether adaptation itself is ablated.
7. Record latency, compute/energy cost, failure handling and uncertainty where reported.
8. For generative/conversational systems, record grounding, hallucination/error evaluation, spatial correctness and safety/privacy handling.
9. Record artifact/reproducibility state using the review-wide R model.
10. Assign M0-M7 without promoting technical sophistication to a higher maturity state.
11. Permit “adaptive benefit” only when the adaptation mechanism is isolated against an appropriate comparator.
12. Permit “field-ready” only with intended-user operational evidence.
13. Permit “generalizable” only with cross-context or replication evidence.
14. Otherwise QUALIFY or HOLD the claim.
