# Algorithm — Industrial AR Deployment Evidence Gate

Input: industrial AR study s and claim c
Output: I0-I6, evidence flags, PROMOTE/QUALIFY/HOLD

1. Identify industrial process and intended operational decision/task.
2. Identify participants: students, researchers, novices, trained workers, maintenance professionals, operators.
3. Record whether equipment/task is simulated, representative, or operational.
4. Record device, PPE compatibility, lighting/noise/mobility/connectivity and environmental constraints.
5. Record AR function and content-authoring/update burden.
6. Record integration with PLM/MES/CMMS/ERP/DT/IoT or other workflow systems where relevant.
7. Separate task metrics, worker outcomes, system metrics, safety and organizational outcomes.
8. Record exposure duration and repeated use.
9. Record deployment scope: lab, pilot, line/site, repeated operation, multi-site.
10. Record failures, downtime, maintenance and support burden.
11. Assign I0-I6 from demonstrated deployment evidence.
12. Do not promote student laboratory performance to worker productivity.
13. Do not promote a one-off field demonstration to organizational adoption.
14. Do not call a system scalable without integration/authoring/support evidence.
15. PROMOTE only claims supported at their deployment tier; otherwise QUALIFY or HOLD.
