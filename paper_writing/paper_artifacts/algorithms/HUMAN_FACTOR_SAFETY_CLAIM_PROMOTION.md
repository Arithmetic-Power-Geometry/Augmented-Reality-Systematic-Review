# Algorithm — Human-Factor Safety Claim Promotion

Input: candidate safety claim c and evidence set E
Output: PROMOTE, QUALIFY, or HOLD

1. Identify the safety goal: training, operational assistance, situation awareness, cognitive state, physical burden, behavioral risk, or adaptation.
2. Record exposure duration and whether the setting is simulated, laboratory, realistic-task, field, repeated field, or sustained deployment.
3. Separate system/model performance from human safety outcomes.
4. Separate immediate task performance from retention/transfer.
5. Record device/display and mobility conditions.
6. Record subjective, objective, physiological and behavioral measures separately.
7. Check comparator and baseline compatibility.
8. Check whether benefit and burden were both measured.
9. If aligned evidence directly supports the claim, PROMOTE.
10. If evidence supports a narrower device/context/outcome/time horizon, QUALIFY.
11. Otherwise HOLD.

No generic statement that AR is safer is permitted without a defined safety outcome and context.
