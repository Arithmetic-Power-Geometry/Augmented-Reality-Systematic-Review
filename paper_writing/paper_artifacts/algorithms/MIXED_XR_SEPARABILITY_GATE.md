# Algorithm — Mixed-XR Separability Gate

Input: secondary or primary evidence item e
Output: AR_SEPARABLE, XR_CONTEXT_ONLY, or EXCLUDE_AR_SYNTHESIS

1. Identify modalities explicitly studied: AR, MR, VR, XR-unspecified.
2. Determine whether AR participants, systems, outcomes, or studies can be separated from non-AR modalities.
3. If AR-specific evidence is extractable, classify AR_SEPARABLE and retain its AR conditions.
4. If the item informs context/theory but AR outcomes cannot be separated, classify XR_CONTEXT_ONLY.
5. If conclusions depend entirely on VR or non-AR modalities, classify EXCLUDE_AR_SYNTHESIS.
6. Never transfer pooled XR effects to AR without separability.
7. Record device, interaction, population, task, comparator and outcome conditions before cross-study comparison.
