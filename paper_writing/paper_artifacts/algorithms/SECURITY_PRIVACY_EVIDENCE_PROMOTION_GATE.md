# Algorithm — Security/Privacy Evidence Promotion Gate

Input: candidate claim c, supporting studies E
Output: PROMOTE, QUALIFY, or HOLD

1. Identify the protected asset and affected actor(s).
2. Identify attacker/threat assumptions and attack surface.
3. Separate demonstrated attacks from hypothesized risks.
4. Separate implemented mitigations from conceptual proposals.
5. Record device, environment, application and multi-user context.
6. Record security/privacy outcome and usability/performance cost.
7. Check whether compared studies share compatible threat models and metrics.
8. Check independent replication or multiple-study support when the claim is generalized.
9. If evidence directly supports c under aligned conditions, PROMOTE.
10. If evidence supports only a narrower context, QUALIFY the claim to that context.
11. Otherwise HOLD; do not convert an empty cell or speculative threat into a verified gap.

This is an evidence-synthesis procedure, not a new cybersecurity algorithm.
