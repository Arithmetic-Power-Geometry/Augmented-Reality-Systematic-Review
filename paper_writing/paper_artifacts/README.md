# Paper Artifacts

This folder is the single manuscript-facing artifact workspace.

## Rule

Raw evidence and immutable source records remain in their audit directories. Every table, figure, algorithm, analysis summary or derived dataset intended for manuscript writing is copied or generated here with provenance back to the raw evidence.

Subfolders:
- `tables/` — manuscript-facing tables and machine-readable table data.
- `figures/` — generated figures and their source data.
- `algorithms/` — final algorithms/pseudocode used by papers.
- `analysis/` — analysis summaries and statistical outputs.
- `provenance/` — mapping from manuscript artifact to generating evidence/code/commit.

Nothing in this folder may convert a discovery candidate, hypothesis or unrun experiment into a finding.
