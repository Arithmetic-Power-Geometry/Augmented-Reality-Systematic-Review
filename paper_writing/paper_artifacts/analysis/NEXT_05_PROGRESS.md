# NEXT-05 Progress — Systematic Corpus Expansion and Freeze

## Current state
NEXT-05 is **IN PROGRESS**, not complete.

The project now has:
- 16 screened seed studies from Batch 1;
- 6 additional high-value primary candidates in Batch 2;
- a validated master bibliography expanded from 4 to 8 entries;
- a pre-run workflow audit that corrected two concrete repository errors.

## Why the corpus is not frozen yet
The frozen protocol requires execution of the registered source-query families across the declared bibliographic sources, deduplication, title/abstract screening, full-text eligibility, citation chasing and a process-based stopping rule.

A general web search cannot honestly substitute for source-specific IEEE Xplore, ACM DL, Scopus, Web of Science, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink and PubMed export logs. Therefore:
- no fabricated database result counts;
- no fabricated export checksums;
- no fabricated PRISMA totals;
- no claim of search exhaustion.

## Parallel progress
While formal source-query completion remains open, targeted discovery is filling weak workstreams (edge/cloud, tangible interaction/occlusion, accessibility, collaborative DT, human factors) and every fully validated paper is appended to the master BibTeX.
