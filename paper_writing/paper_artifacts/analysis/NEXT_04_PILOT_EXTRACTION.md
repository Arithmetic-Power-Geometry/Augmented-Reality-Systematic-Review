# NEXT-04 Pilot Extraction Analysis

## What the extraction demonstrates

The 16 screened seed studies can be represented in the common evidence architecture without erasing their methodological differences. The seed contains technical system evaluations, controlled human experiments, accessibility studies, physiological workload measurements, industrial action research and security experiments.

## Immediate analytical signals (not final field claims)

1. **Metric heterogeneity is structural.** Security attack success, localization performance, cognitive workload, usability and industrial task outcomes are not commensurable. This supports the need for task-family comparability rather than a global leaderboard.
2. **Hardware is part of the evidence context.** HoloLens variants recur, but other records use spatial AR, Gear VR, smartphones/LiDAR, mobile AR and platform-level AR frameworks.
3. **Human evidence needs stratification.** Seed populations include children, disabled users, industry operators and general participants; aggregation without population/context strata would be misleading.
4. **Reproducibility cannot be inferred from open access.** Full-text availability is separate from code/data/config/evaluator availability. Unknown fields remain explicitly unknown.
5. **Reported findings remain source-level evidence.** They are not converted into our own causal or prevalence conclusions.

## Extraction maturity

Four records currently have strongly validated bibliographic metadata in the master BibTeX and three have core reported findings independently cross-checked in authoritative records. The remaining records are coded conservatively from verified discovery/full-text metadata and retain `unknown` or `pilot_partial` where deeper full-text extraction is still required.

This conservative status is intentional: completeness is not manufactured.
