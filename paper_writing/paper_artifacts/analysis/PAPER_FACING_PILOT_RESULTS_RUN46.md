# Paper-Facing Pilot Results — Run 46

## Seed evidence flow
Sixteen discovery records were canonicalized without a bibliographic duplicate in the seed. All 16 passed title/abstract eligibility and entered structured extraction. This seed flow is a workflow-validation corpus and is not the final PRISMA population.

## Multidimensional evidence
The P_i representation accommodated all 16 studies while preserving differences among security evaluation, multimodal interaction, accessibility, cognitive workload, industrial collaboration, digital twins, localization and education. Hardware and sensing were not ancillary metadata: HoloLens variants, spatial/video see-through systems, smartphones, LiDAR/GNSS, EEG and eye tracking materially changed the evidential context.

## Comparability
Direct cross-study ranking was not defensible across the seed. The closest thematic families remained predominantly C1–C2 because apparently similar studies differed in task, device, user population, environment, protocol or metric semantics. No C3 pair is asserted from the present extraction.

## Reproducibility
Artifact evidence was substantially less complete than bibliographic/full-text access. Code availability is explicitly recorded for S0001, whereas many seed records retain unknown code/data/configuration fields. Consequently, the pilot does not compute a final reproducibility-rate statistic. The result supports treating transparency, executability, reproduction and replication as separate states.

## Contradictions and failure regimes
The pilot exposes candidate tensions rather than verified contradictions: AR guidance versus cognitive cost; accessibility benefits versus interface/context barriers; digital-twin/operator benefits versus deployment constraints; and localization capability versus environment/sensor dependence. Each candidate changes conditions across studies, so formal contradiction testing must be condition matched.

## Gap promotion
CG001 comparability, CG002 reproducibility separation, CG003 cognitive-load heterogeneity and CG005 accessibility/context specificity are observed in the seed. CG004 collaboration is seed-supported but not persistent, and CG006 intelligent/LLM AR remains untested by this pilot. None is promoted to a verified opportunity.

## Boundary
These results validate the analytical machinery and provide defensible examples for Methods/Discussion. They do not replace the final formal-corpus Results.
